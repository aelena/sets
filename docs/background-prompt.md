# Recap background prompt

The photos in `src/assets/backgrounds/` were generated with this prompt. The app draws all text itself, so the images must carry none.

```
Vertical 9:16 photograph, 1080×1920, for use as the background of an Instagram
story. Moody, cinematic, natural light. Subject: [SUBJECT]. Shallow depth of field,
soft film grain, muted colours with one warm accent.
Low-key lighting, deep blacks, industrial textures (concrete, steel, brick, rubber flooring);
any people appear only as out-of-focus shapes in the background.

Composition: the subject and the interesting detail sit in the upper half of the
frame. The lower 60% is calm and fairly dark (floor, wall, shadow or soft bokeh)
with no strong detail, because white text and numbers will be laid over it. Keep
the top 15% simple as well.

No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names or watermarks anywhere in the image.
No recognisable faces. Photorealistic, not illustrated.
```
